# RENZ S9A-P Signature Experience Inventory

**Status date:** 8 September 2026  
**Scope:** client-preview visual elevation only  
**Selection rule:** every score is favourable: `5` means highly RENZ-specific, highly relevant/impactful, strong on mobile/accessibility, low runtime cost, or straightforward to maintain. `1` means weak, generic, inaccessible, expensive, or complex. Selection is based on fit across the whole system, not on a raw total alone.

S9A-P needs one memorable construction language, not a collection of unrelated tricks. The candidate register therefore evaluates 48 possibilities and admits 12 coordinated moves. Preview studies remain server-gated, explicitly non-evidentiary, noindex/nofollow, and removable without changing the published-content architecture.

## Selected system

The 12 selected moves are intentionally interdependent:

1. Once-per-session construction opener.
2. Responsive provisional RENZ mark/lockup.
3. Current-route datum navigation.
4. Full-screen architectural navigation index.
5. Three-depth Hero 2.0 with continuous datum.
6. Five-member architectural field family.
7. Provisional three-chapter Expertise experience.
8. Monument / Split / Drawing-Mass project study family.
9. Normal-scroll project story with sticky chapter rail.
10. Accessible Drawing → Reality range interaction.
11. Measured native-disclosure process timeline.
12. Final-datum footer with reversed mark and discreet staff access.

The testimonial track and contact/location plane are implemented as presentation architecture within the same native/CSS system, rather than counted as additional signature effects. This keeps the memorable interaction count bounded while satisfying the preview’s proof and contact demonstrations.

## Rejected technology posture

- No Motion runtime: the coordinated states are achievable with CSS keyframes, native dialog/disclosure/range controls, and two narrow React client islands.
- No GSAP/ScrollTrigger: normal document flow, native anchor links, static progress styling, and sticky positioning provide the required story without pinning or hijacking.
- No Three.js or React Three Fiber: no approved scene, evidence, or performance case exists.
- No pointer-chasing field: the route-specific static field family is stronger, cheaper, and works equally well on touch and reduced-motion paths.

## Kill criteria

Any selected move is simplified or removed if it delays meaningful content or focus, creates horizontal overflow, obscures a control, survives in preview-off mode when it contains study material, increases CLS, produces a serious axe violation, or cannot resolve to a complete static reduced-motion state.
