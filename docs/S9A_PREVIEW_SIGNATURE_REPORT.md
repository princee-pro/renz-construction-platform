## 4. Provisional RENZ logo system

`ProvisionalRenzMark` reconstructs the recognizable structural blue R, grey vertical mass, gold roof/diagonal/datum, and window register from the byte-preserved 190 × 181 client raster. Six variants exist: full, compact, micro, horizontal, reversed, and one-color. The component admits 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, and 96-pixel symbols; tests cover all six variants at every required 16–48-pixel size, and compact/micro remove the window detail.

This is an approximate, preview-only derivative—not an auto-trace, approved vector, trademark master, or silent replacement. The complete provenance, hash, missing approvals, and production promotion gate are recorded in `S9A_PROVISIONAL_BRAND_MARK.md`.

## 5. Brand opener

The homepage opener assembles registration, vertical mass, provisional R, and gold datum across 1.36 seconds. Semantic page content and the primary CTA already exist underneath; the overlay waits for no asset and has no network dependency. Its final mark position creates an intentional visual continuity approximation toward the separate header mark rather than claiming one shared DOM element.
