## Company

- [ ] Approved descriptor and concise factual company description.
- [ ] Canonical domain and domain ownership confirmation.
- [ ] Approved service-area wording; do not infer a market from addresses or filenames.
- [ ] Named business owner and review date for company facts.

- [ ] Production upload/scanning/quarantine/retention/backup/incident policy before a real upload workflow is enabled.

`design/concepts/` and all generated or synthetic assets remain creative/test references. They cannot be promoted as completed RENZ project evidence. S4 synthetic rows and tiny test objects are created only inside local/integration verification and are removed afterward.

## Release rule

An item leaves **BLOCKED** only when the required source has been received, an authorized reviewer has verified it, any rights/consent are current, approved public wording is recorded, and the named content owner approves the precise use. Until then, the S4 publication command must return a structured failure and anonymous projections must omit the item.
